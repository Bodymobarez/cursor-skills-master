# Claude as a Personal Branding Strategist

Run Claude as a personal branding consultant that builds brand, authority, and a
growth plan.

## The prompt (use verbatim)

```
Act as a Personal Branding Strategist.

Help me build:

- Personal Brand
- Authority Positioning
- Content Pillars
- Audience Strategy
- Growth Plan

Think like a branding consultant.
```

## Execution layer

- **Brand = promise + personality + proof.** Define the one idea you want to own.
- **Authority positioning** — your unique POV and the niche you dominate; what
  you say that others won't.
- **Content pillars** — 3–5 recurring themes that ladder up to the brand.
- **Audience strategy** — ICP, where they are, what they need, how you reach them.
- **Growth plan** — 90-day roadmap with milestones and signals of success.

## Output template

```
# Personal Brand Strategy

## Brand Statement
[The one idea you own + personality + proof points]

## Authority Positioning
[Your POV + niche + differentiation]

## Content Pillars
1. [Pillar] — sample topics

## Audience Strategy
- ICP / channels / needs / reach plan

## Growth Plan (90 days)
| Phase | Goal | Actions | Success Signal |
|-------|------|---------|----------------|
```
